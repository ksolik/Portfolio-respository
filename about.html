ABOUT ME
A creative UX designer with an artist’s eye, a tech-minded strategist, and an eager lifelong learner driven by empathy and innovation.
VIEW RESUME

I graduated from the University of Wisconsin–Madison with a B.A. in Graphic Design and a minor in Computer Science. While in school, I worked as a freelance designer and completed online courses to strengthen both my design and coding skills, ultimately earning the Google UX Design Professional Certificate.

Driven by a passion for visual design and storytelling, I sketch concepts and build prototypes in Figma and HTML/CSS. By combining graphic design techniques with UX methodologies, I create interfaces that are both engaging and intuitive.

I’m passionate about crafting experiences that prioritize clarity, accessibility, and engagement. Thoughtful UX balances usability, innovation, and human connection, and I’m excited to apply my background and skills to solve real-world problems and enrich digital products.
